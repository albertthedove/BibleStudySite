What the dates show. Galatians to Romans (AD 49–57) were all written before any Gospel, even on early datings. The prison letters and the Pastoral letters (AD 60–67) overlap with Mark, usually seen as the earliest Gospel (c. AD 60–65). Matthew, Luke and John are probably later. James may be earlier than any of Paul’s letters. Dates are approximate and follow a conservative scholarly view; some scholars place several books later.
