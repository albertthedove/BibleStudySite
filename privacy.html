Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026.

VizBible is a static website. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms, and it does not set cookies or run analytics or advertising of its own.

What other services can see

  • Maps. When you open a map, your browser may request map tiles from Esri (ArcGIS Online). Those services receive your IP address and the area you are viewing, under their own privacy policies.
  • Place images. Photos in map pop-ups are loaded from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons when you open a place, and they receive your IP address too.
  • Links out. Bible reference links open BibleGateway, and other links open sites we do not control. Their policies apply once you leave VizBible.
  • Hosting. The site host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and reliability.

Donations

If a donation option is added, payments will be handled by the payment provider. VizBible does not see or store your card details.

Contact

If you write to us, we will use your message only to reply and will not share it. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
