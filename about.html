About VizBible

VizBible turns the Bible’s timelines, journeys and numbers into pictures you can explore. It is built for students, pastors, teachers and anyone who wants to see how Scripture fits together: where Paul travelled, what Jesus did in each place, which letters were written when, and how the kings of Israel and Judah followed one another.

What you will find

  • Paul’s journeys and Jesus’ journeys, with ancient and modern place names and Bible references for every stop.
  • A New Testament timeline of all 27 books, with the churches Paul wrote to.
  • The kings of Israel and Judah, books by date of writing and a book-by-book dashboard.

About the dates and places

Dates follow widely used conservative chronologies, and ranges are shown where scholars disagree. Routes on the maps are approximate, and some sites are disputed; the maps say so where that is the case. Please check the Scripture references for yourself. The aim is to honour the Word of God with careful, reverent presentation.

Sources

Place data and Bible references come from the Bible itself. Base maps use Natural Earth and Esri. Place images, where shown, come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons.
